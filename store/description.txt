EDITH is a voice assistant for your G2 that lives on the display. Hold the ring and ask, or turn on hands-free and just say "EDITH, ...". Answers type out on the HUD in a dry, to-the-point voice, with Google Search for anything current.

ASK ANYTHING
News, prices, scores, flight status, quick facts. Maths, tips, bill splits and unit conversions answer instantly, even offline.

GET AROUND
Walking directions with a turn arrow and street names. Nearby cafes, pharmacies and transit departures. "Remind me to buy batteries when I get to the shop." "I parked here", then later "take me back to the car". Ask "what plane is that?" when one flies over.

STAY ON TOP OF THE DAY
A dashboard with time, weather, timers, reminders and your Google Calendar. Heads-ups when rain is coming, when to leave for an event, and who you're about to meet. Lists you tick off with the ring. A daily briefing in the morning and a debrief at night.

CONVERSATIONS
Live captions and translation, with rewind. Meeting mode records, transcribes and saves a summary with action items, plus a speaking coach: your pace, filler words and how much of the talking you did.

ON STAGE AND IN THE KITCHEN
A teleprompter that follows your voice and turns the page as you read. Recipes and how-tos one step per screen, with a timer for each step. Flashcards with spaced repetition.

AND MORE
Spending log ("I spent 12 pounds on lunch"), smart home control through Home Assistant or IFTTT, an emergency screen with the nearest hospital, police and pharmacy, a walk and run tracker, three personalities, and a phone remote that mirrors the display.

BRING YOUR OWN KEYS
EDITH uses free tiers: paste your own Gemini and Groq API keys into the phone app. Keys and data stay on your phone.
