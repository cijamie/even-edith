EVEN EDITH

Connecting to glasses…

Glasses

A live copy of the display. Use the controls to drive the glasses while someone else wears them.

API keys

Your keys stay on this phone and are sent only to Google and Groq. Both have free tiers.

Preferences

Home city is used for weather and as the map fallback when location is off.

Teleprompter

Say "EDITH, open teleprompter" and swipe through it on the glasses.

Recipes and guides

Paste a recipe here, or just ask: "EDITH, walk me through pancakes". Swipe for steps; press to start a step's timer.

Lists

Notes

Meeting summaries and activity logs land here too. Share sends one to email or messages.

Google Calendar

Show your Google Calendar on the glasses, with heads-ups before events, and add events by voice.

Memory

Places

Location reminders fire when you arrive, while EDITH is open. Pins are dropped by voice, and automatically at the start of walks and navigation.

Spending

Flashcards

Press to flip on the glasses, swipe down for "got it", up for "again". Cards come back on a spaced schedule.

Smart home

"EDITH, lights off." Uses webhooks from Home Assistant Cloud or IFTTT.

Emergency

Backup

Move EDITH to a new phone, or keep a copy before reinstalling.

Connection test

Checks each service from this exact app. Run it inside the Even app on your phone: that's the environment that counts.
