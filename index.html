Recipes and guides

Paste one here, or ask "Jarvis, walk me through pancakes". Swipe for steps; press for a step's timer.

Google Calendar

Your calendar on the glasses, heads-ups before events, and new events by voice. Also powers "what plane is that?".

Smart home

"Jarvis, lights off." Uses webhooks from Home Assistant Cloud or IFTTT.

Backup
